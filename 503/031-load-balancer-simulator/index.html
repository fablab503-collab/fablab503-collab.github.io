Load Balancer Simulator

Jobs arrive with a load in percent. The load balancer sends each job to the device with the lowest current utilization. Utilization is the sum of the loads of the jobs running on a device. This is a local simulation only — everything runs in this page, no server, no network.

Add a device

A device is a worker with no fixed limit — its utilization is the sum of the loads of the jobs it is running, shown as a percentage.

Load = the utilization percentage the job adds to its device while running. Duration = how many time units the job needs.
