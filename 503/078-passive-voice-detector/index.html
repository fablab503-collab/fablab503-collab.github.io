Passive Voice Detector

Rule-based pattern matcher. It flags likely passive constructions and highlights them below.

    How it works: any form of “to be” (is, was, were, been, …) followed by a past participle (a word ending in -ed, or a common irregular like “written”, “taken”, “given”) is flagged. It is a heuristic, not a grammar proof — short sentences like “He was tired” are not flagged because “tired” is not in the participle list.
