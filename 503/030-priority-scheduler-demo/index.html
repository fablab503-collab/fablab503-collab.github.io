Priority Scheduler Demo

Preemptive priority scheduling: when a high-priority job arrives, it interrupts (preempts) the running lower-priority job. The interrupted job keeps its remaining time and resumes later. This is a local simulation only — everything runs in this page, no server, no network.

Add a job

Arrival = the time the job appears. Burst = the time units it needs. A lower priority number runs first; on a tie, the earlier arrival runs first.
