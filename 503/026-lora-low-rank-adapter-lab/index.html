LoRA Low-Rank Adapter Lab

Full fine-tuning updates every number in a weight matrix W (m × n). LoRA keeps W frozen and trains a small low-rank update instead: ΔW = B × A, where A is r × n and B is m × r. Move the rank r and watch the parameter count change.
