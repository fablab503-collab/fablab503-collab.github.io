    Who I am

    I learned every job I have done by doing it. A café in Austria taught me to run a team of ten, buy, price and plan. A railway site taught me safety and heavy machines. A FabLab at Institut d'Optique taught me to turn a student's idea into a real object in a day.

    Since 2025 I build software the same way: I start, I ship, I fix. I use AI agents as my team, and I understand how they work, where they fail and how to check them. With them, one person can do the work of a small studio: in 2025-2026 I designed and built about 40 apps and tools, one of them live on the French App Store.

    I am looking for a team in Montpellier or remote where I can build real products, as an employee or as a freelancer.
